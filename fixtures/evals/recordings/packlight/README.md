# Packlight phone recordings

Raw recordings of the Packlight fixture app (`scripts/evals/packlight-fixture.mjs`), an
Expo app running in the iOS Simulator, recorded by the agent itself in a script-to-video
eval (attempt `5485c038`, 2026-10-31): the iPhone screen with its status bar and
Dynamic Island, 1206 × 2622, as a founder's phone recordings would look.

- `1-open-trip.mp4` (11.4 s): the trips ("Good trips start with a little list."), then
  A weekend away opens: 2 of 6 packed.
- `2-add-and-tick.mp4` (31.1 s): "Travel adapter" typed and added, then Phone charger
  ticked; the count goes to 3 of 7. Long, with slow typing.
- `3-share.mp4` (24.1 s): Share packing list; the iPhone share sheet opens over the
  list, then closes.
