Connect your companion

Use the PostOrca desktop app, or a compatible companion installed on this computer.

PostOrca uses com.postorca.browser. The standalone example uses com.postorca.browser.example. Your companion developer supplies its host name and installation instructions.

The selected companion can read page content, capture screenshots, and act using your signed-in sessions in tabs opened by the bridge. Its operator determines whether that data is sent to servers or AI providers. Only connect software you trust and review its privacy policy.

Changing companions ends the current connection. Previously opened bridge tabs close when the next connection is established.
