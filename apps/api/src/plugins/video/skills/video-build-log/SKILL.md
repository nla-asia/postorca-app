Read video-create. Aim for 15–25 seconds on X/Reels/TikTok or up to 40 on LinkedIn, cutting every 2–2.5 seconds. Open with the biggest change or result, not a monthly-update title. Give each metric, launch or improvement its own beat and exact on-screen number including units. Before/after pairs are particularly clear. Add implications or next steps only when supplied, then close softly with an invitation to follow progress.

Do not round, combine or derive new percentages. Speak numbers naturally and show digits. Avoid filler summaries, brand-only scenes and hype. Show work around the metrics: desks, calls, notebooks or whiteboards without fabricated readable charts. Reuse images with different metric stickers. Before export, check every supplied number appears exactly and aligns with its spoken sentence; average scenes stay below 3 seconds.
