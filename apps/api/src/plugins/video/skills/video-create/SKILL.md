description: "Read for every short video: scripting, UGC shots, media tools, previews and MP4 delivery."
---

Compose short videos from saved images, recorded clips, text, speech, captions and optional music. Check desktop_status and the video plugin's availability before generating assets for a desktop render. Default to native phone-style Reels/TikTok/Shorts; follow a requested landscape demo or verbatim brief instead of imposing UGC styling. Also read the skill for the video type: product-content-edit, video-pov-storytime, video-build-log, video-lesson, video-product-update, video-event-invite or video-post-to-video. Read video-music whenever using music.

Write the script before making images. Use first person, short everyday sentences and about three words per second: roughly 45 words for 15 seconds, 90 for 30. Prefer 15–30 seconds for Reels/TikTok, up to 45 for LinkedIn; stay below 60 unless requested, with an absolute limit of 90 seconds. If the first take is far too short, one new take may use more supplied material or speed 0.9, never filler.

Open immediately with specific tension, surprise or a supported claim, in the first spoken sentence and the opening sticker. A product name or title alone is a weak hook. Avoid greetings and introductory scene-setting. Develop one idea, pay it off, then use a soft close. Mention the company naturally and late unless announcing it. Put URLs on screen, not in speech. The destination platform is not automatically the topic. Preserve exact names, numbers, links and dates; never invent results, quotations, actions, emotions or personal details. Leave unsupported details out and describe what the founder could add. Spell times and abbreviations naturally in speech; keep digits in on-screen text.

Plan cuts every 2–3 seconds: about eight shots for 20 seconds, twelve for 30. Consecutive scenes using one image form a continuous shot, not separate cuts; each clip scene is a shot of its own. Usually generate 3–6 images and alternate/reuse them (A/B/C/A/B). Use the supplied screenshots and recordings when available; a recorded clip beats a screenshot of the same screen. The video plugin already includes the media generation tools, so no plugin switch is needed.

For speech, list voices without search first (multiword search can return nothing); choose a conversational voice from its descriptions, without inferring the founder's gender. If no voice was specified, choose one once and mention the choice. Generate the script. For portrait imagery use 1024x1536, candid smartphone photography, natural light and real settings unless the brief asks otherwise. Avoid stock poses, generated readable interface text and generated faces presented as the founder. Hands, a soft over-the-shoulder view or the setting work well. Leave calm areas for stickers and captions. Immediately note each returned image ref, its content, intended beat and defects; images are available in model context only briefly. Revise existing images with media_edit_image. Never invent file refs or voice IDs, or automatically repeat failed paid calls.

Build scenes in order with duration_seconds, image_file and optional text. A scene can instead play a saved video with video_file (muted): start_seconds and end_seconds trim it, speed (0.5–4) speeds up waits and dull stretches, and hold_seconds freezes the last frame. Omit duration_seconds for a clip and it lasts the trimmed part at its speed plus the hold; a duration_seconds longer than that holds the last frame, a shorter one cuts the clip. Trims must stay within the clip's length (screen_record_stop returns duration_seconds). fit, device, focus and highlight work on clips, with regions as fractions of the video frame; look at preview frames of a clip before choosing them. Use text-only cards sparingly and never as the default opener. The first scene carries the hook; subsequent stickers belong on roughly one scene in three, for a number, step, lesson or call to action. Keep them around eight words or fewer, sentence case; avoid duplicating captions. Explicit line breaks are preserved and long words shrink.

Pass the speech ref as voiceover_file; the server transcribes it once for timing and captions, so do not call media_transcribe separately. Keep captions enabled unless asked otherwise. The first preview returns voiceover_lines: align cuts and stickers with these phrase starts. Fix timing by adjusting durations, not regenerating speech unless its words need correction. The final scene extends to the voiceover plus 0.6 seconds when needed. Otherwise end around a second after speech, treating brief lengths as approximate. Brand colours load automatically; omit brand unless an override was requested. Mention missing saved brand colours instead of inventing them.

Use video_preview_frames at 2–8 chosen times, including the opening. Inspect the hook, important steps, ending, exact wording, legibility and whether text blocks relevant content. Record observations by time in that response. Fix actual errors and preview only changed times; avoid endless minor timing adjustments. Export with video_export using the reviewed arguments and a short title. Save the actual output with create_artifact, kind video, content its ref. Describe format, length, shots, voice, music and any unresolved issue. Frame checks do not establish sound or full-motion quality; never claim you watched or listened without doing so.

Revisions reuse saved inputs and change only requested details, preserving earlier exports. Variations change angle, hook, beat order and script, not just the opening line; reuse images and deliver each video separately. Never publish. On availability/disconnect failures, check desktop_status once and stop if still unavailable. An export timeout has an unknown outcome: do not rerun it to test connectivity. Report other render failures honestly. Before delivering, read references/review.md with read_skill.
