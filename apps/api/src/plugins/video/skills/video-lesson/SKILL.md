name: video-lesson
description: "A founder lesson, backed by the supplied story, or a numbered set of lessons."
---

Read video-create. Aim for 20–30 seconds on Reels/TikTok or 30–45 on LinkedIn with 2–3 second cuts. Open on the cost or contrast that makes the lesson worth hearing, not a generic lesson title. Tell what happened in two to four beats, show supplied numbers, then the realization, a short on-screen lesson close to the brief, and optionally a directly supported application. End with a question or soft follow invitation.

Tell the story in first-person past tense and the lesson in present tense. Do not invent an anecdote to support a thin brief or preach at the viewer. For N lessons, give each its own numbered scene and sticker; the hook establishes why they matter. Show the relevant setting, put numbers in overlays rather than fabricated dashboards, and reserve the strongest shot or a text card for the lesson. Check exact numbers, a hook that stands alone and lesson text synchronized to the spoken line.
