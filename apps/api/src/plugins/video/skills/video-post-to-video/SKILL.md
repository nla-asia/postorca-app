---
name: video-post-to-video
description: "Turn a supplied written post into speech while preserving its sentences and claims."
---

Read video-create. Keep the post's sentences in order. Whole sentences or clauses may be cut to fit; minor spoken smoothing is allowed, such as & to and. Add no claims, sentences or calls to action. State any cuts in the summary. Do not speak hashtags, emoji or links; a closing link may be shown.

Estimate about 2.5 words per second, up to 30 seconds for Reels/TikTok or 45 for LinkedIn. Cut on clauses/sentences every 2–3 seconds, avoiding flicker below roughly 1.5 seconds. Speak the opening sentence first; a sharper later line may serve only as the opening sticker. Use exact key phrases for about three or four stickers: hook, turn and ending, usually under eight words. Show the post's real subject and reuse one image per idea. Check script sentence order and exact quoted overlays before export.
