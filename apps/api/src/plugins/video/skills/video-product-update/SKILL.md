Read video-create. Prefer 15–25 seconds for Reels/TikTok, up to 35 for LinkedIn. Open on the concrete frustration that led to building it, then explain the supplied context and cost. Show one to three benefits as what someone gets. Introduce the exact product/feature name after the problem, and end with the supplied URL or availability detail on screen.

Use first person without hype or invented results/users/features. Show the problem and then the calmer outcome. Generated screens must remain soft, never fabricated interfaces; disclose stand-ins and suggest actual screenshots. Verify the problem-first opening, exact name/URL and source support for every spoken claim.
