---
name: video-product-demo
description: "Demonstrate the product through real screens and steps, with a result-first hook."
---

Read video-create. A demo shows how the product is used, in the order a user meets its steps. For a thin brief, choose the platform's usual duration, first-person narration and captions; explain these defaults. Prefer 15–25 seconds for Reels/TikTok, up to 35 for LinkedIn, around 8–10 shots for 20 seconds.

Open on the supported payoff or pain removed, spoken and on screen. Put when/where it is used next. Give every supplied step a beat of 2–3 seconds, describing what someone does or gets; use short 2–5 word step labels. Then show the outcome and close on the exact URL or name. Say and show the product name at least once; never speak its URL. Avoid inventing personal usage beyond what the founder supplies.

Use real screenshots whenever possible. Read all supplied screenshots once and note their refs, screen type, existing frames, visible features and useful region boxes as fractions x/y/width/height. Visible controls are evidence; unsupported results, prices or feelings are not. Skip empty/setup screens unless they support a beat. Obtain available screenshots with existing browser tools or read_file; if access is missing, explain what the founder must supply rather than pretending capture happened.

Choose 9:16 for a named Reel/TikTok/Short or phone app. For desktop screens, prefer 16:9 for YouTube/LinkedIn or square/4:5 for feeds unless the brief specifies otherwise. Show a whole desktop screen once, then focus or highlight each relevant control in portrait videos. fit contain preserves a whole screenshot. Device browser/laptop/phone frames are for bare screen content only: never double a screenshot's own window frame. Focus boxes should contain complete elements and labels; highlights retain location context while dimming the rest. Consecutive scenes may walk through regions on one screenshot. Limit stickers to 2–4 meaningful beats and keep them clear of the interface.

When real screens are unavailable, generated scenes may show hands, a soft screen, the setting or use around the product. Never fabricate its UI. Vary framing; disclose stand-ins and recommend real screens. Check every step's timing and region in previews, keep average shots below 3 seconds (at most 3.5 except the closing beat), and verify the final name/URL exactly. Avoid product-name openers, long feature slides, stickers over controls and narration of irrelevant example data.
