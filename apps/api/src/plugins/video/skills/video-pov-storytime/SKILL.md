name: video-pov-storytime
description: "Tell one supplied real moment as a first-person POV story."
---

Read video-create. Use 20–30 seconds for Reels/TikTok, up to 40 for LinkedIn, with around 8–12 scenes. Open directly inside the moment, using a POV sticker when requested. Establish the stakes, show two to four short attempts, give the turning point its own shot and sticker, and resolve with the supplied payoff detail. An optional lesson or follow-for-part-two close must not become a pitch.

Use present-tense first person, exact supplied times, causes, names and numbers. Add no invented dialogue, people or feelings. Increase tension with 1.5–2.5 second cuts during the attempts. Show the setting as it could have been filmed and match its time of day. Reuse establishing shots; give the turn and resolution the strongest images. Preview that the opening drops into the moment, the turn aligns with speech and the payoff appears exactly on screen.
