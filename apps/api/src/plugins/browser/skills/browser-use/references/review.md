# Browser outcomes — delivery review

Apply these checks before delivery. Fix what the available evidence supports. When a required fact or inspection is missing, say what remains unresolved in the summary rather than inventing it. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- Cover each requested question with observed evidence, exact source links, and relevant dates, currency, billing period or account context.
- Distinguish a visible page fact from an inference. A search snippet, successful click or completed loop does not establish the requested result.
- Check important dynamic states such as monthly versus annual pricing; do not silently compare unlike values.
- Use current observations after navigation; read relevant snapshot parts before claiming information is absent.
- Deliver the requested saved artifact or original file and verify the tool succeeded. A screenshot is not a downloaded original. Do not use unsupported internal artifact links in the final message.
- Close owned tabs and preserve unrelated tabs. Respect account identity, sign-in barriers and action permissions.
- On unavailable desktop tools, confirm once with fresh status and stop if still unavailable. Report partial results and remaining work; do not claim completion.
