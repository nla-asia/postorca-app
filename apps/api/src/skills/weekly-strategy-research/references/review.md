# Strategy report — delivery review

Check the report before delivering it and fix what fails. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- Last week's list was read from memory; some founders were kept if still active, and new ones were added.
- Six founders in the founder's space: two much bigger, two similar, two smaller than the founder. Founders or builders, not media accounts or companies.
- Every number was seen or counted over the last 7 days; anything missing is "–", estimates are marked "≈".
- The founder's own row comes first, with the same numbers as the others.
- The gap is stated in plain words with numbers, and the week has one focus area and exactly three actions, each tied to a gap and saying how often.
- The report reads in about two minutes. No guessed pronouns, nothing invented.
- This week's list and numbers are saved to memory, keeping at most four weeks.
