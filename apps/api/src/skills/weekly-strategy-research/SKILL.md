# Weekly strategy report

Show the founder how their effort on one platform compares with founders like them: are they posting and replying enough, and how does their reach compare. This is a reminder and a nudge for the week, not a growth analysis. Keep it short enough to read in two minutes.

## The founders to compare with

Six founders in the founder's space, of different sizes, compared with the founder's own account:

- two with far more followers (about ten times or more),
- two of similar size (about half to twice),
- two with fewer (about half or less).

They are founders or builders who post about the founder's topics themselves, not media accounts, companies or celebrities.

**Keep the list between weeks.** Read `x/strategy-peers.md` (or the file for this platform) from your memory first. Keep some of last week's founders who are still active, typically three or four, and find new ones for the rest. Repeats are fine; the same six forever is not. Drop anyone who stopped posting or turned out not to be a founder in this space.

**Finding new ones.** Search for posts the way founders in this space actually write, not just the profile's topic labels. Turn each topic into the phrases people use in posts and bios: for "startup operations and automation", try phrases like "building in public", "solo founder", "shipped", "automating my", "AI agents for", "first customers", "MRR". Use the platform's recent and top filters. From posts that fit, look at the authors: their bio says what they build, and their profile shows their followers. Check their size against the founder's before choosing.

## Counting

For the founder and each of the six, over the same window: the 7 days ending today, including today. State the dates in the report so weeks compare like with like. From each person's own profile:

- **Followers**, as shown.
- **Posts a day**: their original posts in the last 7 days, divided by 7. Reposts don't count.
- **Replies a day**: their replies in the last 7 days (on X, the Replies tab), divided by 7. If someone replies far more than you can scroll through, count what you saw, note the date span it covered, and estimate the week from that, marked with "≈". Only estimate from a span of at least half a day; a burst in one hour says nothing about a week, so write "–" instead. For a timeline too busy to count posts, search `from:handle since:YYYY-MM-DD` and count the results.
- **Average views and likes** per post, over their most recent original posts (up to ten), from the numbers shown under each post.

Only use numbers you saw or counted. If one isn't visible, write "–" rather than guessing. On sites that show who viewed a profile (LinkedIn), follow the browser skill's rule and use only what posts and search results show.

## Process

1. Activate the browser plugin and read its skill. Open the account's own page and read it. If it is signed out or shows a different account, stop and say so.
2. Read last week's list from memory.
3. Count the founder's own numbers.
4. Recheck the founders you keep from last week, find the new ones, and count everyone's numbers.
5. Close the tabs you opened.
6. Save this week's list to memory (see below) before delivering.

## Deliver

Create one document artifact titled `Strategy — <platform> — <date>`:

1. **Where you stand**: one or two sentences with the founder's own numbers.
2. **Compared with founders like you**: a table with the founder first, then the six (handle linked to their profile, size group, followers, posts a day, replies a day, average views, average likes). For founders kept from last week, show their follower change.
3. **The gap**: two or three plain sentences on where the founder's effort and reach differ most, for example "Founders your size post twice a day and reply about fifteen times; you post once every three days and reply about once a day."
4. **This week**: one focus area (the biggest gap), and exactly three concrete actions, each tied to a gap: what to do and how often ("reply to five founders in this list every weekday").

Refer to the founder by name or as "you"; never guess pronouns. The content is the report only, with no notes about how you wrote it. To fix it, revise it with edit_document. Make sure every tab you opened is closed, then end with a one-line summary.

## Memory

Keep one file per platform, `x/strategy-peers.md` for X, with the description "Founders compared in the weekly X strategy report, with their numbers by week". Under a heading for this week's date, list the founder's own row and each of the six: handle, size group, followers, posts a day, replies a day, average views, average likes. Keep the last four weeks and remove older ones, so next week can show who grew. Edit the file in place rather than starting a new one.
