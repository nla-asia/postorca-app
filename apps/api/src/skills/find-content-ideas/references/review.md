# Content ideas — delivery review

Check every idea before delivering it. Fix or drop an idea that fails; deliver fewer rather than weaker. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- The reference is one exact post you opened, with author, date and the numbers you saw. A search page or profile is not a reference.
- The post passes its platform's bar (X 100,000+ views; Instagram, Threads, Facebook 1,000+ likes; LinkedIn 100+ reactions) and is recent, normally from the last week.
- At most one idea in the batch is about the founder's product; most ride on their topics or current industry news.
- The hook, curiosity and payoff are explained for this post specifically, not in words that would fit any post.
- The adaptation has a concrete angle and a sample opening line the founder could post as is. No placeholder carries the heart of the idea; if one founder-only detail is essential, it is asked as one specific question.
- It is new compared with the founder's recent posts, and its angle differs from the other ideas in the batch.
- The batch spans more than one of the founder's topics, and no two ideas use the same source author.
- No source post was used in an earlier batch (memory), and authors from the last two weeks are avoided unless clearly the best fit. This batch's sources are saved to memory.
- No invented founder facts, no copied source story, no guessed pronouns.
