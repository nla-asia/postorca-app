# Daily quote — delivery review

Check the quote and image before delivering and fix what fails. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- The account's page was checked for a post today, in the founder's local date; if there was one, nothing was drafted.
- The quote is short, relatable to the founder's audience, sounds like them, and isn't a cliché, political or a product mention.
- It is the founder's own line, or a known quote with a verified original source and exact attribution.
- It isn't a repeat from the last 90 days, and today's quote is saved to memory.
- The image shows exactly the quote (and attribution), spelled right and easy to read, in the founder's saved quote style.
- Nothing was published or engaged with.
