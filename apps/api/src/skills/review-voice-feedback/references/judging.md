# Judging feedback: worked examples

Each example is one founder message and what voice.md already holds, then the call and why.

| Founder said | voice.md already has | Call | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "it was 4 opens, not 3" | anything | Nothing | A fact fix. |
| "last month, from a Stripe export" (answering a placeholder) | anything | Nothing | A detail only they know, for this post. |
| "make this one about pricing instead" | anything | Nothing | The topic of one post. |
| "since it's launch day, add a bit of hype" | Tone: blunt, no hype | Nothing | Tied to this post ("since it's launch day"). |
| "I never use emojis, drop it" | nothing about emojis | Rule, strong | A standing preference ("never"). Add a Rule and a Never does line. |
| "drop the emoji" | Never does: no emojis | More evidence | Orca broke an existing entry: no new entry; say in the summary that the draft broke it. |
| "cut the second line, it's too long" | nothing about length | Note | A clear correction that would apply to any post. Seen once. |
| "too long again, keep the second line short" (next day) | Note: keep the second line short (seen once) | Note to Rule | The same lesson on a different day. Update the Shape variable. |
| "too long" in two tasks today | nothing | Rule | Seen in two tasks the same day. |
| "meh" / "not quite" / "make it better" | anything | Nothing | Nothing specific to learn. |
| "too stiff" | nothing | Signal | A direction but not specific yet. Hold it; a second one on another day becomes a Note. |
| "write it longer, more of a story" | Rule: keep posts to two lines (seen 3 times) | Signal, named in summary | A single weak message against a strong Rule: don't flip the rule on one request. |
| "less formal, write like I talk" (second day) | Signal: "too stiff" (2026-10-27) | Note | Two signals in the same direction on different days. Remove the signal. |
| "no hashtags on LinkedIn" | Platform differences: none for LinkedIn | Rule, strong, LinkedIn only | A standing preference scoped to one platform; keep it to LinkedIn. |
| "end on the point, not a question" | their X posts often end on a question | Note, X, flagged | Still their wish for drafts, but their own posts differ: keep the scope and say so in the summary. |
