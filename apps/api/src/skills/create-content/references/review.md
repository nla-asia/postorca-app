# Posts — delivery review

Check the post before delivering it and fix what fails. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- The post is complete and built on one real detail from the founder: an actual event from memory or their posts (not an opinion they hold), or one placeholder for it. Deleting the detail would break the post. At most one other small placeholder. Each placeholder gets one specific, easy question in your final message.
- The first line stops the scroll and makes sense on its own; two alternative openings are listed below the post.
- It makes a clear point the founder can own. No line would fit on any founder's feed unchanged.
- It keeps the idea's angle and hot topic, and the founder's note where given.
- Platform limits hold: X posts at most 280 characters each (or a numbered thread), LinkedIn about 100–250 words; no hashtags on LinkedIn, at most one on X; emojis only if the founder uses them.
- It sounds like the founder's recent posts on this account.
- The company appears only where it truly fits. No invented facts, no copied source story, no guessed pronouns.
- When the founder gave a story, it is saved to `stories.md` in memory and the post reads as theirs, not a filled template.
- A revision changes only what was asked, still fits the platform limits, and leaves one current document. Never publish.
