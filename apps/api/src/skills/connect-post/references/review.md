# Connect post — delivery review

Check the post before delivering it and fix what fails. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- No connect-style post went out on the account in the last 7 days; otherwise nothing was drafted.
- It says specifically what the founder builds or works on, and names who they want to meet.
- Its angle and opening differ from the last four connect posts in memory, and this one is saved there.
- It ends with an easy reason to reply.
- It fits the platform (X at most 280 characters, no hashtags on LinkedIn), sounds like the founder, has no link or pitch, and invents nothing. Any placeholder is small and asked about in your final message.
- Never engage on the account.
