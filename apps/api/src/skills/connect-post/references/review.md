# Connect post — delivery review

Check the post before delivering it and fix what fails. Keep this checklist out of the deliverable itself.

- No post inviting new people to connect went out on the account in the last 7 days (thank-you posts don't count); otherwise nothing was drafted.
- It is only about connecting: no build log, day count, launch, demo, milestone or feature news.
- It says who the founder is in a few casual words, with no company tagline or product description, and names who they want to meet.
- Its angle and opening differ from the last four connect posts in memory, and this one is saved there.
- Who they want to meet is a short list of two to four groups, each a few words.
- It ends with exactly one call to action: a question or an invitation, not both.
- It fits the platform (X at most 280 characters, no hashtags on LinkedIn), reads like the founder's recent posts rather than a landing page, has no link or pitch, and invents nothing. Any placeholder is small and asked about in your final message.
- The other openings each work with the rest of the post as written.
- Memory notes use the founder's name or "they", never a guessed pronoun.
- Never engage on the account.
