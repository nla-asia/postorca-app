# Connect post

Draft one post that tells people who the founder is and invites the right people to connect. For a small account, an honest "here's what I'm building, who I'd love to meet" post brings followers who share the founder's interests and gives them a reason to talk. It only works when it is specific and not too frequent, so each one takes a different angle from the last. The founder reviews and publishes it.

## Requirements

- **Says who they are, specifically.** What the founder is building or working on, and their topics, in their own words from the brand profile and recent posts. "Building an AI agent that writes release notes" beats "building in AI".
- **Names who they want to meet.** A clear group: people building in the same space, a role, people working on the same problem. The right people should recognize themselves in one read.
- **A fresh angle each time.** Rotate between angles, and never reuse the angle or opening of the last four connect posts (your memory keeps them; see Memory):
  - what they're building right now and what's next
  - what they're stuck on or learning, and who could help
  - a small win or milestone, and who they'd like to share the next one with
  - a question to people in their space ("what are you building this week?")
  - their interests outside the product that their audience shares
- **An easy reason to reply.** End with a simple invitation: reply with what they're building, say hi, or answer the question. On X, "Let's connect" plus one line about who should reply is enough.
- **Fits the platform.** X: one post of at most 280 characters (aim for 250 or fewer), at most one hashtag such as #buildinpublic if the founder's posts use hashtags. LinkedIn: about 60–150 words, short paragraphs, no hashtags. Threads: one short post. Emojis only if the founder's recent posts use them. Follow the account's publishing instructions in the brand profile.
- **Sounds like the founder**, not like a template: match how they write in recent posts.
- **Not an ad.** The product can be what they're building, but no link and no pitch.
- **Never invents facts.** No made-up numbers, milestones or stories. Where one detail only the founder knows would make it stronger, use one small marked placeholder, such as [this week's milestone], and ask about it.

Refer to the founder by name, or as "they". Do not guess pronouns.

## Process

1. Activate the browser plugin and read its skill. Open the account's own page from the brand profile and read it. If it is signed out or shows a different account, stop and say so. Note the account's follower count.
2. Read the last connect posts from memory: `<platform>/connect-posts.md`, for example `x/connect-posts.md`.
3. Read the account's recent posts. If the founder already posted a connect-style post in the last 7 days, don't draft another: end with one line saying so, with its link.
4. Pick the angle that fits what the founder is doing now and hasn't been used recently, and write the post. Read it once as a stranger in the founder's space: if it would fit anyone's feed, make it more specific.
5. Close the tabs you opened.
6. Save the post's angle and opening to memory before delivering (see Memory).

## Deliver

Create one document artifact titled `Connect post — <platform> — <date>`. Its content, in this order:

1. The post itself, ready to paste once any placeholder is filled.
2. **Other openings:** two alternative first lines.
3. **Placeholders:** each placeholder in the post, if any.

Add no notes about how you wrote it. In your final message, link the document and ask one specific question for each placeholder; if there are none, say it is ready to post. When the founder answers or asks for changes, revise the same document with edit_document (read_artifact first, since they may have edited it).

**Bigger accounts.** This routine helps most below about 1,000 followers. If the account is above that and your memory has no note that you already said so, add one line to your final message: the follower count, and that the founder can pause this routine in Routines if they no longer need it. Save that you said it in the same memory file, and don't repeat it.

Never publish, reply, like, repost or follow. The founder reviews and posts.

## Memory

Keep one file per platform, `x/connect-posts.md` for X, with the description "Connect posts drafted for X: angle and opening by date". Under a heading for today's date, note the angle and the post's first line. Keep the last eight entries. Edit the file in place rather than starting a new one.

## Delivery review

Before delivery, read `references/review.md` with `read_skill` and apply its checks alongside this skill.
